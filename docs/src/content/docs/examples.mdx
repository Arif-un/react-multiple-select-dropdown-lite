## Groups

Groups have `type: 'group'`, a `title` and `childs`, and can nest. Options themselves can't have `type: 'group'` or `type: 'submenu'`. `emptyDataLabel` shows when a group has no options. With `collapsible: true`, a click on the title (or Enter) shows and hides the group's options; `collapsed: true` starts it closed. Search looks inside groups, closed ones too.

<Preview code={groupsCode} title='Groups.tsx'>
  <Groups client:visible />
</Preview>

## Submenus

With `type: 'submenu'`, the title is a row that opens its `childs` in a popover beside the menu: on a pause over it, a click, Enter or Arrow Right. With the highlight in it, Arrow Left or Escape close it again. Submenus nest to any depth and can hold groups. Search lists their options flat.

## Group titles

`renderGroupTitle` renders the title rows of groups and submenus from `{ group, title, total, selected, open, icon }`: `total` counts the options in it, nested ones too, and `selected` those picked. `icon` is the expand icon, for you to place or leave out.

<Preview code={groupTitlesCode} title='GroupTitles.tsx'>
  <GroupTitles client:visible />
</Preview>

For an icon of your own, leave `icon` out and pick one from `open`. Push it to the end yourself: the stylesheet's auto margin is on `.msl-expand-icn` only. A submenu is `open` while its popover shows, so check `group.type` to keep its icon pointing right:

```jsx
renderGroupTitle={({ title, total, selected, open, group }) => (
  <>
    {title}
    <span>{selected}/{total}</span>
    {open && group.type !== 'submenu' ? (
      <ChevronDown aria-hidden className='ms-auto' />
    ) : (
      <ChevronRight aria-hidden className='ms-auto' />
    )}
  </>
)}
```

The stylesheet doesn't turn or mirror your icon, so it is up to you right to left.

## Option objects

Options can be any shape: `getLabel` and `getValue` read them. `onChange` gets the selected objects back as its second argument. Values created by `customValue` are always `{ label, value }`. The fields `disabled`, `style`, `classes` and `type` keep their meaning in any shape.

## Fixed values

`fixed: true` on an option keeps it once selected: its chip has no delete button, and Backspace, the clear button and the menu leave it. In single select it locks the value. Tint its chip with `--msl-chip-fixed-background`, style `.msl-chip[data-fixed]` (Tailwind's `data-fixed:` on `classNames.chip`), or read `fixed` in a `customChip`.

</Preview>

## Search in the menu

`searchInMenu` puts the search field at the top of the open menu. The closed control shows only the values, or `placeholder` when there are none, with no search placeholder next to the chips. `searchPlaceholder` labels the field. Single select and `disableChip` can then search with a value selected.

## Virtual list

`renderList` renders the rows through a virtual list library of your choice, here [TanStack Virtual](https://tanstack.com/virtual), so only the rows in view render. Every kind of row works: groups in groups, collapsible and collapsed groups, submenus in groups and groups in submenus, an empty group, and 11,000 options in a collapsed group and a submenu. See [virtual lists](../virtual-lists/) for react-window and other libraries.
